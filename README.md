# tva.services

Personal portfolio and services site for Tatiana Vasilchenko — AI & Computer Vision Engineer.

Live at: [https://tva.services](https://tva.services)

## About

A static one-page site presenting AI/LLM consulting and computer vision services, with a contact form and the legal pages required for an EU/Spain-facing business site (Legal Notice, Privacy Policy, Cookies Policy).

## Stack

- Plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step
- Design based on [Strata](https://html5up.net/strata) by [HTML5 UP](https://html5up.net), used under the [CCA 3.0 license](https://html5up.net/license)
- Hosted on GitHub Pages with a custom domain (see `CNAME`)
- Contact form submissions are relayed by email via [FormSubmit](https://formsubmit.co)
