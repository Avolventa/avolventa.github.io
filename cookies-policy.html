Cookies Policy
Política de Cookies

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites can store on your device to remember information about your visit. Similar client-side technologies, such as your browser's local storage, can serve comparable purposes.

2. Cookies used on this website

tva.services does not currently use tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies of any kind. The only mechanism in use is your browser's local storage, which remembers whether you accepted or declined the cookie notice, so it does not need to be shown to you on every visit. No personal data leaves your browser through this mechanism, and it is not shared with any third party.

3. Third-party cookies

This website does not currently embed any third-party services (such as analytics or advertising networks) that would set their own cookies. If that changes in the future, this policy will be updated in advance and, where required, your consent will be requested before any new cookie is set.

4. How to control cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting them. Refer to your browser's help section for instructions. You can also update your choice for this website at any time using the button below.

5. More information
